SOBA Solidarity Scheme

Supporting one another through unity, brotherhood and collective responsibility.

About the Scheme

The SOBA Solidarity Global Group (SSGG) brings together eligible SASSE alumni across Europe and the United Kingdom to provide financial solidarity support to the bereaved family of a member.

Our guiding principle is simple: One Brotherhood, One Support System.

€50 One-time registration fee
€15,000 Target bereavement benefit
Shared Contribution per approved claim

Membership Eligibility

Membership is intended for eligible SASSE alumni within the scheme's geographical scope.

Registration requires alumni verification, payment of the registration fee, and administrator approval.

Once approved and activated, members receive access to their secure membership portal.

How Contributions Work

When an eligible bereavement claim is approved, the required contribution is shared among the members eligible to contribute to that claim.

Contribution per member = Approved benefit amount ÷ Number of contributing members

Example: With 1,000 contributing members and a €15,000 benefit, the contribution is €15 per member for that approved claim.

The actual contribution depends on the approved benefit and the number of eligible contributing members.

Bereavement Support

The scheme aims to provide a €15,000 bereavement benefit following the death of an eligible member, subject to the applicable scheme rules and claim approval procedures.

Claim verification, beneficiary confirmation and payment authorisation will be handled through the scheme's administration process.

Membership Administration

Approved members will be able to use their secure portal to view membership information and, as additional features are introduced, contribution records and payment status.

Contribution collection and claims processing are not yet enabled on this public website.
