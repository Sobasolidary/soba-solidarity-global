About SOBA Solidarity

SOBA Solidarity Global Group is a solidarity initiative designed to bring together eligible Sasse alumni through an organized membership and mutual-support system. Our mission is to strengthen brotherhood, promote collective responsibility and provide a structured framework for supporting members and their families.

Brotherhood

Strengthening the lifelong connection between members of the Sasse alumni community.

Solidarity

Encouraging collective support and shared responsibility among members.

Accountability

Maintaining an organized membership system with alumni verification and administrative oversight.

How Membership Works

Membership applications follow a verification, payment confirmation and approval process.

Register Online

Complete the registration form with your personal information, SASSE class year, matriculation number and next-of-kin details.

Alumni Verification

Your alumni information is reviewed against available SASSE records.

Payment Confirmation

Pay the official one-time registration fee. An administrator confirms the payment.

Membership Activation

After verification, payment and approval, your membership is activated and you can access your member portal.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can register?

Registration is intended for eligible Sasse alumni. Applications are subject to alumni verification.

How much does registration cost?

The one-time registration fee is €50.

When does my membership become active?

Your membership becomes active after alumni verification, payment confirmation and administrator approval.

How do I access my membership card?

Once approved, use your member login credentials to access your dashboard and digital membership card.

Why is next-of-kin information required?

It helps the organization maintain an appropriate contact record for membership administration and solidarity-related matters.
